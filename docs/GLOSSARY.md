# FulfillOps — Glossary

## 3PL
Third-party logistics provider.

## SKU
Stock Keeping Unit.

## WMS
Warehouse Management System.

## TMS
Transportation Management System.

## Fulfillment
Allocating, picking, packing and preparing goods for delivery.

## On-hand
Quantity physically recorded as present.

## Reserved
Quantity committed to active fulfillment.

## Available
Quantity still eligible to be reserved.

## Inbound
Goods entering a warehouse.

## Receiving
Verification and recording of arriving goods.

## Putaway
Moving accepted goods into storage locations.

## Picking
Retrieving inventory from storage.

## Packing
Grouping picked items into packages ready for shipment.

## POD
Proof of Delivery.

## Inventory Ledger
Auditable sequence of stock movements/changes.

## Allocation
Selecting warehouse/location stock to satisfy demand.

## Reservation
Committing available stock to a fulfillment request.

## Tenant
An independent organization using the SaaS platform.

## Tenant Isolation
Guarantee that one tenant cannot access another tenant's data.
